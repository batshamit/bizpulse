Intelligence for the Modern Enterprise

BizPulse is a multi-agent AI platform designed to transform raw business data into actionable marketing strategies with rigorous validation and zero hallucination.

The Core Philosophy

In an era where AI can often hallucinate or provide generic advice, BizPulse takes a different approach. We believe that business intelligence must be grounded in evidence, isolated for security, and vetted by a council of diverse perspectives.

Council of LLMs

Every analysis is performed by a collaborative "Council" of models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI. This diversity ensures that no single model's bias or failure mode dominates the output.

Grounded Citations

Every factual claim made in our reports includes direct citations back to the source data. If the AI can't prove it, we don't show it.

Tenant Isolation

Your data is yours alone. Our architecture enforces strict tenant isolation at the retrieval layer, ensuring that your business insights never leak across boundaries.

Industry Verticalization

Generic marketing doesn't work. We use specialized "Industry Packs" that customize the analysis pipeline for Fintech, Real Estate, Construction, and more.

How it Works

  1. Ingestion: We crawl and process your business website and related public data.
  2. Retrieval: Relevant context is retrieved using high-dimensional vector search.
  3. Analysis: Multiple specialized agents (Scouts, Critics, Strategists) debate the data.
  4. Synthesis: A final Judge agent consolidates the debate into a clean, actionable marketing strategy.
